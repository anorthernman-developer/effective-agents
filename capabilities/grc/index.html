Governance, Risk & Compliance

Ship Outcomes, Not Chat

Govern agents like production systems: identity, Purview, audit, eval gates, and kill-switches.

Problem

What breaks first

Agents appear faster than owners, policies, and audit trails. Shadow bots answer from the wrong library. Boards hear “AI” and think risk theatre — or freeze everything.

Approach

How we cut through

Treat every agent as a named production system: owner, identity, grounding bounds, action gates, preference review, and a kill switch. Align language to NIST AI RMF categories (map, measure, manage, govern) without drowning operators in paperwork.

What we do

Concrete work, not brochure fog

  • Estate inventory (Scout / catalogue patterns) and ownership RACI
  • Purview / DLP / audit paths verified before scale
  • Human gates for irreversible actions; eval sets as release brakes
  • Incident and escalation playbooks that prefer completion over cover-ups
