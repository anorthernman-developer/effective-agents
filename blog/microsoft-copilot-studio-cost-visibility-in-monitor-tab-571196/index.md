# Microsoft Copilot Studio: Cost visibility in Monitor tab

**Ship Outcomes, Not Chat** — a short read on a Microsoft 365 roadmap item.

## What it is

Microsoft Copilot Studio: Cost visibility in Monitor tab (Copilot Studio, Copilot, Agent). Roadmap id `571196`.

## What it does

Now makers will have clear visibility into their consumption costs directly within Monitor tab at both a per-agent level and an estimated breakdown of those costs across the agents lifecylce. This will allow makers to see costs distributed across activities such as authoring,…

## What to worry about

Cost visibility without outcome metrics can become another chat bill. Tie spend to completed work.

## What it means for users

Makers and CoE leads get clearer levers on agent lifecycle cost and behaviour — use them to prove effectiveness, not just publish bots.
